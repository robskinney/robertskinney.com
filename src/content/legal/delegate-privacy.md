Delegate is a mobile app for viewing and working with your [Manager.io](https://www.manager.io) business. It is published by Rocket Tutoring LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

We don't collect, store or sell any of your data. Delegate has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app talks only to the Manager server you connect it to.

## Information stored on your device

- **Server addresses and access tokens.** When you add a business, Delegate saves the server URL and the access token you enter. Access tokens are kept in your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore-backed storage on Android).
- **Cached business data.** To start quickly and work with a poor connection, Delegate keeps a copy of recently viewed data from your Manager business (such as invoices, contacts and bank balances) in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Manager website sign-in.** If you sign in to your Manager website inside the app (for example, to view attachments or printable documents), the session cookie is stored by the app's built-in browser on your device, the same way a web browser would store it.
- **Preferences.** Display settings, such as your Summary screen layout, are stored on your device.

Deleting a business from the app removes its token and cached data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Information sent over the network

Delegate sends requests directly from your device to the Manager server you add, whether that is your own Manager Server or Manager Cloud. Those requests include your access token so the server can identify your business. We do not operate any server in between and never receive this data.

Your Manager server's own privacy practices apply to the data it holds. For Manager Cloud, see Manager.io's privacy policy.

## Sharing and exporting

When you share or print a document from Delegate, it is handed to the destination you choose through your device's standard share or print sheet.

## Children

Delegate is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
