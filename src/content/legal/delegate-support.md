Delegate is a native iOS and Android client for [Manager.io](https://www.manager.io), for both self-hosted Manager Server and Manager Cloud.

## Contact

Email rockettutoring@outlook.com with questions, bug reports or feature requests. Please include your device model, operating system version and Delegate version (shown in Settings).

## Getting started

1. In Manager, open your business and go to **Settings → Access Tokens**, then create a token.
2. In Delegate, tap **Add Business** and enter your server's address (for example `https://yourserver.example.com`) and the access token.
3. Add more businesses the same way and switch between them from the business menu at the top of any tab.

## Common questions

**Delegate can't connect to my server.** Check that the server address opens in a web browser on the same device, and that the access token hasn't been deleted in Manager. On iPhone and iPad, servers outside your local network must use `https://`.

**Why can't I see my other businesses automatically?** Manager access tokens belong to one business, so each business needs its own token.

**Where is my data stored?** See the [privacy policy](/delegate/privacy).
